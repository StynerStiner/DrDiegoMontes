Dr. Diego Montes, D.C. is a bilingual (English/Spanish) family wellness chiropractor at In8Life – Tressler Chiropractic in Export, Pennsylvania. He's passionate about helping families restore their natural ability to heal through corrective, nervous-system centered chiropractic care—without relying on drugs or surgery.

  • Bilingual Care (English & Spanish)
  • International Experience (U.S. & Mexico)
  • Nervous-System Centered Care
