About Chiropractic Care

Chiropractic care is a healthcare profession focused on diagnosing and treating musculoskeletal disorders, particularly those affecting the spine. Dr. Montes uses hands-on spinal manipulation and other alternative treatments to properly align the body's musculoskeletal structure, enabling the body to heal itself without surgery or medication.
Most patients experience little to no discomfort during adjustments. You may hear popping or cracking sounds, which is simply gas being released from the joints. Many patients report feeling immediate relief and increased mobility after treatment. Dr. Montes uses gentle, precise techniques tailored to your comfort level.
Chiropractic care effectively treats back pain, neck pain, headaches, sciatica, sports injuries, posture problems, and joint pain. It also helps with overall wellness, stress reduction, and improving mobility. Dr. Montes creates personalized treatment plans based on your specific condition and health goals.
The number of sessions varies depending on your condition, severity, and overall health. Some patients experience relief after just a few visits, while chronic conditions may require ongoing care. During your initial consultation, Dr. Montes will assess your condition and recommend a personalized treatment plan with an estimated timeline.
Yes, chiropractic care is extremely safe when performed by a licensed professional like Dr. Montes. It's a non-invasive, drug-free approach to healthcare with minimal risk. Serious complications are very rare. Dr. Montes conducts thorough evaluations to ensure chiropractic care is appropriate for your specific condition.
Absolutely! Chiropractic care is safe and beneficial for children of all ages. Dr. Montes uses gentle, age-appropriate techniques for pediatric patients. Children can benefit from chiropractic care for growing pains, sports injuries, posture issues, and overall wellness support during their developmental years.

Appointments & Insurance

You can schedule an appointment by calling us at (724) 327-5665, emailing contact@in8life.com, or using the contact form on our website. We're open Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm. We offer flexible scheduling to accommodate your busy lifestyle.
We work with most major insurance providers. Please contact our office with your insurance information, and we'll verify your coverage and benefits. We also offer affordable self-pay options and flexible payment plans for those without insurance coverage.
Your first visit includes a comprehensive consultation, health history review, physical examination, and diagnostic assessment. Dr. Montes will discuss your symptoms, concerns, and health goals. If appropriate, you may receive your first adjustment during this visit. The initial appointment typically takes 45-60 minutes.
We're located at 1 Technology Ln, Export, PA 15632. Our office hours are Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm. We're conveniently located and offer ample parking for our patients. Contact us to schedule your appointment during these hours.
In most cases, you don't need a referral to see Dr. Montes. Chiropractors are primary care providers, and you can schedule directly with our office. However, some insurance plans may require a referral for coverage. We recommend checking with your insurance provider to understand your specific plan requirements.
